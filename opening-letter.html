Founding Note / CUT TO

CUT TO started with a simple realization.

After covering concerts, festivals, and interviewing artists across Asia, we found ourselves thinking less about the stage and more about the people behind it.

The stories they shared. The experiences that shaped them. The ambitions, struggles, and moments that brought them to where they are today.

Over time, we realized those stories were what stayed with us the most.

So we created CUT TO.

A platform for conversations with artists, creators, entrepreneurs, and cultural voices across Asia.

Some stories belong to people already in the spotlight. Others come from people creating, building, and inspiring in quieter ways.

We hope these conversations spark curiosity, offer new perspectives, and inspire people in their own journeys.

Beyond the image. Into the story.
