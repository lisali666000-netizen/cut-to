People & Process

Conversations

We’re interested in what happens beyond the release — how people think, make, collaborate and change.

CUT TO is building a growing series of conversations with artists and creative voices across music and culture: musicians, directors, designers, founders and people shaping what comes next.

Our founders, Xuanyi Li and Lisa Chen, bring a shared background in artist interviews, live and cultural reporting, photography and visual storytelling. Their previous work includes conversations with Big Ocean and NoSo.

We’re drawn to process, identity, collaboration and the ideas behind the work. Our approach brings thoughtful English-language editorial coverage together with web storytelling, social-native presentation and a strong visual point of view — always with care for the person at the centre.

The first CUT TO conversations are being prepared. This space will grow into an archive of interviews.
